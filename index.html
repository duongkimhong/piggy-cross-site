Piggy Cross

A cute little road-crossing game

Help a tiny pig get home! Tap anywhere to hop across the road, wait for a gap between the cars, and make it to the barn. Every level adds more lanes and faster traffic.

Coming soon to Android

Features

  • One-tap controls, easy to learn and quick to play
  • Endless levels that get gradually harder
  • Day, dusk and night, with sun, clouds, rain, snow and fog
  • Collect coins after every level
  • Free to play, supported by optional rewarded video ads

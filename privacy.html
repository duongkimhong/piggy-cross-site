We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect duongkimhong, email address, phone number or precise location ourselves.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App may ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. For data processed by Google, please see the Google links above.
